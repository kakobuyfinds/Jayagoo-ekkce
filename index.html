Jayagoo spreadsheet guide

Build a list you can actually use.

01

Save the source

Keep the product URL, seller name, category and date checked. Record the exact variant instead of relying on a thumbnail.

02

Check the fit

Add the size chart and relevant measurements. Note whether dimensions refer to the garment, the body or another measurement method.

03

Review the evidence

Separate seller descriptions from your own observations. Keep unresolved questions visible, including unclear materials or missing photos.

04

Estimate the total

Record currency, estimated delivery costs and applicable fees separately. Replace estimates with confirmed figures when available.

Jayagoo spreadsheet product list

Compare more than the picture.

A useful product list makes missing information easy to spot before you commit to a purchase.

Suggested worksheet fields for fashion research; this is a planning framework, not live inventory.
Product group Record these details Resolve before buying
Shoes Model description, selected size, size chart, upper and sole descriptions. Does the size chart explain foot length, and are your chosen color and size available?
Hoodies & sweaters Chest width, body length, sleeve length, fabric description and fit notes. Are measurements taken flat, and how do they compare with a similar garment you own?
T-shirts & jerseys Chest, shoulder and length measurements; print or embroidery details. Do the photographs and description refer to the exact selected version?
Jackets & sets Layering allowance, lining, closures and separate top and bottom measurements. Which pieces are included, and can their sizes be selected separately?
Pants & underwear Waist, rise, inseam, stretch information, pack quantity and stated return terms. Are dimensions relaxed or stretched, and do any category-specific return restrictions apply?
Headwear & accessories Circumference, dimensions, adjustment range, materials and fastening details. Are the fit range, included components and care instructions clearly stated?

Jayagoo spreadsheet shopping checklist

  • Save the exact listing and selected product variant.
  • Check measurements rather than relying only on size labels.
  • Review clear photographs of construction and finishing.
  • Verify material claims and any supporting documentation.
  • Read the seller's current delivery and return terms.
  • Mark uncertain details as unconfirmed.
  • Recheck price and availability before ordering.

Jayagoo spreadsheet FAQ

What does Jayagoo spreadsheet mean in this guide?

This guide uses Jayagoo spreadsheet as a search phrase for a worksheet-style approach to organizing fashion finds. It does not provide or verify an official Jayagoo spreadsheet, download or platform integration.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is supplied here. This page provides a comparison framework, category links and a shopping checklist that you can copy into your own worksheet.

Which fields should a fashion shopping spreadsheet include?

Include the product link, category, seller, selected size, measurements, color, listed price, currency, estimated fees, review notes and the date you checked the listing.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check relevant garment dimensions and measurement methods. For footwear, compare the seller's size chart and foot-length guidance.

Do brand illustrations confirm authenticity or availability?

No. The brand-themed graphics on this page are original editorial illustrations, not product photographs. They do not establish stock, authenticity, affiliation or authorization.
